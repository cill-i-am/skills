# Tutorial: learn by doing

Create a lesson, not a production checklist. The author chooses a dependable
learning path; the learner should not have to design their own exercise.

## Writing shape

- State what the learner will practise and the concrete result they will create.
- Establish the starting state, access, sample data, and any cost or cleanup needs.
- Choose one small, reproducible path. Introduce actions in manageable steps and
  show what the learner should notice after each significant action.
- Finish with a visible achievement and name the skills practised. Link to a
  real-world task or conceptual background as a next step.

Favor concrete actions and early results over theory. Briefly orient the learner
when needed; put extended rationale and alternative approaches elsewhere. Avoid
branching into every supported platform or configuration during the lesson.

## Review test

Starting with only the stated prerequisites, can a learner complete the lesson
without making an unexplained choice? Verify the whole journey, not just isolated
snippets. Keep practice data recoverable and separate from live customer data.

Illustrative title: **Import your first sample dataset**. This is a title example,
not a claim that the target product offers imports or sample data.
